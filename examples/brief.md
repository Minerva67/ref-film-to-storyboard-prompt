# Tomo — Product Brief (fictional, for demo use only)

> All content below is invented for a public example. Tomo is not a real product.

## Positioning
**"One turn. One focus."**
Tomo is a desk-top focus timer you set by turning a dial, not by unlocking your phone.

## Product
- A round desk object, about the size of a coffee mug lid (Ø 9 cm, 4 cm tall).
- Matte warm-white body, a single knurled aluminium dial on top.
- A thin LED light ring around the base shows time remaining.
- No screen, no buttons other than the dial. Powered by USB-C (cable exits at the back).
- Companion phone app ("the Tomo app") shows focus history. [TBC: app UI screens to be supplied]

## Selling points (copy approved for on-screen use)
1. **Turn to start.** "Turn the dial. Your focus session starts." — 1 click = 1 minute, up to 60.
2. **See time, not numbers.** "The light ring shrinks as time runs out." — no digits to watch.
3. **Stay off your phone.** "No notifications. No screen. Just the ring."
4. **Know your week.** "The Tomo app counts your focus minutes." — weekly bar chart in app.

## Audience
Adults who work at a desk — remote workers, students, designers — who lose focus to their phone.
Evidence in brief: copy talks about "your desk", "your week", "your phone"; no age or family framing.
